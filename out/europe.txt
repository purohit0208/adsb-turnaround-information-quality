   within-route r at gap < 12 h = -0.024, 95% bootstrap CI -0.038 to -0.009
   69% of the published association is between-route composition; the remainder is carried by
   pairs 16% of which are separated by more than 12 hours, i.e. the aircraft
   left the eight sampled hubs and returned later. These are not consecutive legs.
   CONCLUSION: the European propagation claim is withdrawn.

5. WHY THE INFORMATION-DEGRADATION EXPERIMENTS CANNOT BE REPEATED IN EUROPE
   Degrading an inbound-status signal requires one to exist. OpenSky carries no
   schedules, so the only candidate is the aircraft's own previous ground stop,
   observable for 21,332 of 203,103 turnarounds (10.5%) because only
   eight hubs are sampled. Its association with the outcome is -0.024.
   There is no signal of any strength to degrade, so the core experiment of this
   paper has no European counterpart in this dataset.
